# Revision After Change

Use this reference when a source fact, requested addition, semantic definition,
or evidence result changes after an artifact has been drafted.

## Rebuild Affected Prose

1. Identify the changed semantic source and the authorized edit scope.
2. Find every dependent claim, title, summary, conclusion, evidence statement,
   glossary alias or entry, and paragraph transition in that scope. Treat each
   as invalid until checked against the changed source.
3. Rewrite the smallest complete unit that restores the argument. Do not append
   a compensating sentence to stale prose or preserve a stale glossary entry by
   changing the term to fit one artifact.
4. Re-read the preceding and following transitions from the intended reader's
   knowledge state.
5. Re-read the complete artifact when its purpose, through-line, decision, or
   conclusion changed.
6. Preserve exact contractual or reproducibility values. Recompute comparisons
   before rounding observational values to the precision needed for the current
   decision.

When glossary wording changes, keep term identity separate from semantic truth.
Update aliases, linked entries, and dependent prose only when the semantic
source establishes the meaning and the selected glossary location,
documentation scope, and current edit scope authorize the write. Report every
known out-of-scope dependent with its stale term and semantic source; do not edit
it or silently call it current.

## Typical Change Shapes

- **Local factual addition:** rebuild the paragraph that establishes the fact
  and its adjacent transitions; do not tack the fact onto the end.
- **Changed conclusion:** rebuild the conclusion, summary, title, and any claim
  that prepared the old decision, then read the whole artifact.
- **Updated performance result:** replace the value, comparison base, evidence
  statement, and conclusion that depend on it. Keep correctness thresholds
  exact and observational precision decision-sized.
- **Changed project term:** verify the accepted semantic source, update the
  authorized entry and aliases, revise in-scope prose, and report rather than
  edit any known dependent outside scope.
